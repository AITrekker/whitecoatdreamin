<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Every so often something I have done ends up written about somewhere other than this blog. This is where I keep those.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A week as a page in the Washington State Senate</h2>

<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img loading="lazy" width="847" height="477" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/senate.jpg" alt="Monica standing at the rostrum in the Washington State Senate chamber, holding a gavel, beside Senator Manka Dhingra" class="wp-image-438" /></figure>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In April 2023, during my freshman year at Redmond High School, I spent a week in Olympia as a page in the Washington State Senate, sponsored by Senator Manka Dhingra.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I wanted to do it because I wanted to understand how laws actually get made &#8212; not the version in a textbook, but the real thing, with the arguing and the waiting and the procedure. I wanted to watch floor action in person, and I wanted to meet people my age who were curious about the same things.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pages serve for one week while the Legislature is in session. The job itself is unglamorous and constant: carrying documents across the Capitol campus, delivering messages, helping in offices, working on the chamber floor, and carrying the flags to open each day&#8217;s session. Between errands we went to Page School, where we spent part of every day learning how state government fits together.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">My favorite moment was small. On Monday morning I led the Pledge of Allegiance on the Senate floor, in front of the chamber. I also drafted a mock bill on plastic recycling and its environmental impact, which meant thinking properly about how you turn an opinion into something that could survive a committee.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">What surprised me most was how ordinary the building felt from the inside. The Senate is a workplace. People are tired, and running late, and genuinely trying to get something done.</p>

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>I&#8217;m impressed with Monica&#8217;s eagerness to learn, and I&#8217;m looking forward to the tremendous difference she will make in our community.</p>
<cite>Sen. Manka Dhingra (D&#8209;Redmond)</cite>
</blockquote>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I would recommend it to anyone. You spend a week with people from all over the state, and you come out understanding the Capitol as a place where work happens rather than a building you drive past. If you are <a href="https://leg.wa.gov/learn-and-participate/civic-education-programs/page-program/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">at least 14 and not yet 17</a>, you can apply with a parent&#8217;s permission and a teacher&#8217;s recommendation.</p>
