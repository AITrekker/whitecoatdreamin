<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Monica Gupta is a dedicated high school student with a passion for pursuing a career in medicine, she has found a unique way to combine her love for crafting and her aspiration to make a positive impact on the world. Inspired by a recent crochet workshop where she learned to knit hats for newborns and preemies, she has been driven to start a nonprofit guild with a profound purpose. This initiative aims not only to provide warmth and comfort to precious newborns but also to foster a sense of community and compassion among individuals of all ages. By encouraging others to join her in creating and donating these handmade treasures, she envisions a ripple effect of kindness and generosity spreading throughout the community. Moreover, she is committed to maximizing this impact by partnering with employers to match volunteer hours with financial contributions.</p>
