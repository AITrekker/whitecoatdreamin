<p class="wp-block-paragraph">October was a whirlwind of purpose and passion for me, one that started with a simple summer hobby. Learning crocheting last summer was a delightful turn in my life, thanks to a friend&#8217;s invitation to a crocheting class. Little did I know that this newfound skill would intertwine so beautifully with my role as the President of Emerald City Angels, a junior guild non-profit dedicated to the Seattle Children&#8217;s Hospital cause.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">As October rolled in, I saw an opportunity to fuse my hobby with a greater purpose. Hosting the GiVE campaign&#8217;s fundraising events at Microsoft&#8217;s tech campus was invigorating. But it wasn&#8217;t just about monetary donations; it was about harnessing talents. I found myself teaching eager Microsoft employees how to knit hats for premature babies at the hospital. The click-clack of needles echoed through the halls, weaving threads of compassion.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The fundraising efforts were a blend of volunteer hours and direct contributions. It was incredible to witness the community coming together, driven by a shared desire to make a difference.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">By the end of October, our efforts bore fruit. Through volunteer hours and generous donations, we raised funds that reached a staggering figure of $20K. Every dollar represented hope, and every hat knitted symbolized comfort for the tiny fighters at the Children&#8217;s Hospital.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This October wasn&#8217;t just a month of fundraising; it was a convergence of my passions, an intertwining of hobby and purpose that created ripples of goodwill. The connections forged through crocheting classes culminated in something far greater—the unity to support those in need within our community.</p>
