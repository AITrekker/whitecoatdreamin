<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I had the incredible opportunity to attend <a href="https://med.stanford.edu/medcsi.html">Stanford&#8217;s Clinical Summer Internship program</a>, where I explored a wide range of topics in the medical field. From learning how to suture to understanding the anatomy of the heart, and even spending a day in the life of an orthopedic surgeon, the experience was both challenging and rewarding.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I gained insight into what it means to be a physician-scientist. One of the key skills I developed was how to take detailed notes on patient cases and present them to a group of medical professionals, which was an invaluable experience in understanding the critical thinking required in the field.</p>
<!--more-->




<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Throughout the program, we covered various topics in our lectures, including:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Diabetes</li>



<li>Emergency medicine</li>



<li>Brain anatomy and function</li>



<li>Radiology</li>



<li>Kidney anatomy and function</li>



<li>Heart anatomy and function</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">One of the most memorable aspects of the program was the hands-on experience of dissecting a pig kidney, a sheep brain, and a pig heart. This practical approach allowed me to apply what I learned in lectures to real-world scenarios.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I also gained a deeper understanding of the role of Physician Assistants (PAs) by participating in clinical scenarios. In one exercise, I had to diagnose a &#8220;patient,&#8221; played by a PA, by asking the right questions—a task that taught me the importance of communication and attention to detail in medicine.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Overall, this internship not only expanded my knowledge of the medical field but also solidified my passion for pursuing a career in medicine. This has been my favorite experience of the summer of 2024!</p>
